About Vinko Solutions

I’m Vicente Pina Beti. An engineer obsessed with a problem nobody had solved.

For years I’ve had an idea that wouldn’t let go: the way ink is handled in tattooing has real problems of consistency, hygiene and waste that repeat every session, in every studio, and are accepted as “just how it works”.

Vinko Solutions is the answer: the new standard for tattoo machines. No waste, no cross-contamination and within reach of every artist in the world.

How it started

Four moments that explain it better than a pitch.

The observation

With an engineer’s eye you notice what routine hides: prepared ink that gets thrown away, repetitive steps, inconsistencies that experience has to make up for.

The right question

Not “how do I make a better machine?”, but: which part of the process creates the most waste and risk, and can it be solved at system level?

Real development

Design, iterations, tests and more iterations. One condition: manufacturable, consistent and adding no complexity to the studio.

The goal now

Bring the new standard to every tattoo artist in the world, with no compromise on quality.
