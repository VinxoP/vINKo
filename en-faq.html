FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about the project, its status, and what comes next.

What is vINKo System?

A patented system designed to integrate into tattoo workflows — either inside a machine like VK0, or as an external module for any machine. The priorities: less waste, more hygiene, and more control over the ink.

What is VK0?

The integrated tattoo machine built to work with vINKo System from the ground up, optimising the entire workflow.

Is it for sale?

Not yet. The project is in development. Join the waitlist and you'll be among the first to know when that changes.

Why aren't technical details shared publicly?

The system has an international patent (PCT). We share what's needed to understand the value without exposing the engineering.

Availability

When and how it will be ready.

When will it be available?

When the design and validation are ready for a quality first production run. No fixed date yet — the waitlist gets notified before anyone else.

What's left before it reaches the market?

Design-for-manufacturing (DFM) iterations, testing and validation, and an initial limited pre-series.

Will there be limited units?

Possibly in the early phases. Join the list to be among the first to know.

How do I stay in the loop?

Join the waitlist. We only send updates when there's something concrete to share.

Founder Council

For studios and artists who want to be close to the process.

What is the Founder Council?

A small group of artists and studios providing real feedback to help set design and user experience priorities.

How much time does it take?

Not much. Occasional input when there are important decisions about design or functionality.

Do I need to sign an NDA?

If you want to join the Founder Council or discuss the system in depth, yes. The NDA allows us to share more without compromising the patent.

How do I join?

Fill in the waitlist form and indicate that you're an artist or studio. I'll reach out.

Privacy & communication

What we store and how we use it.

How often will you email?

Rarely. Only when there's real progress. No weekly newsletters, no filler.

Join the list — we'll only write when there's something worth sharing.
