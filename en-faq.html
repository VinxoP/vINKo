FAQ

Everything you need to know about the VK0.

If you can’t find your answer, email us at info@vinkosolutions.com.

The VK0

What is the VK0?

The next-generation tattoo machine, designed and built by Vinko Solutions. It cuts waste and biological footprint to a minimum, reduces workload and improves hygiene and efficiency in use.

What is the “new standard”?

The way we believe every tattoo machine should work from now on: ink in its own cartridge, zero waste and no cross-contamination. See it in The new standard.

How is the ink loaded?

The cartridge comes empty: you fill it with the ink you prefer and fit it in the machine. When the session is over, the disposable tube is removed and a new one is used.

Will there be pre-filled cartridges?

That’s our next goal: for ink manufacturers to sell their inks pre-filled in cartridges for VK0, with the ink regulated from the source.

Who is it for?

Any tattoo artist and any studio, anywhere in the world. Our goal is to put the new standard within everyone’s reach.

What’s in the kit?

The VK0 machine, the pedal, cartridges and a rugged case. Contents may vary.

Availability

Is it on sale?

Not yet. Right now we are testing it with artists and studios through the pilot program.

When will it be available and how much will it cost?

We’ll announce it first to pilot participants and the waitlist.

Pilot program

How can I test the VK0?

By applying for a place in the pilot program. We review every application and, if you’re a fit, we’ll suggest a short chat and a test session.

Do I have to sign anything?

Yes, a non-disclosure agreement (NDA) before the test.

How much time does it take?

Very little: a call, a test session and your feedback.

Is it a pre-sale?

No. Taking part carries no commitment to buy.
