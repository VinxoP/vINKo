Quick answers

About the pilot program.

How much time does it take?

Very little: a short chat, one test session and your feedback. No weekly meetings.

Do I have to sign anything?

Yes, a non-disclosure agreement (NDA) before the test.

Is it a pre-sale, or am I committing to buy?

No. The pilot program is neither a pre-sale nor a purchase agreement: it’s taking part in the product’s development.

Where does the test take place?

We arrange it with you based on your location and unit availability. If you’re outside Spain, apply anyway: we’ll be opening new locations.

How many places are there?

A few per phase. We value feedback quality over volume; if you don’t get into this phase, you stay on the list for the next one.
