The problem

Tattoo machines have evolved. The way we use ink hasn’t. Caps filled “just in case” and fresh ink for every client: whatever is left ends up in the bin.

01

Waste

More ink is poured than used so you never run short. When the session ends, the rest goes in the bin.

02

Hygiene that costs ink

To avoid cross-contamination, every client starts with fresh caps and fresh ink. Whatever was left from the last one is thrown away.

03

Workload

Preparing, refilling, cleaning and throwing away. Time you don’t spend tattooing.

What we’ve done

This is how every tattoo machine should work.

At Vinko Solutions we have raised the tattoo machine to a new standard: ink goes in its own cartridge, nothing is wasted and there is no cross-contamination. And we want it within reach of every tattoo artist in the world.

Explore the VK0

Part by part.

Tap the dots on the exploded view or pick a part from the list.

Part 1 of 6

Cartridge

A cartridge designed exclusively for the VK0 machine, where you can load the ink that best suits your sessions.

Part 2 of 6

Disposable tube

It connects to the cartridge and runs towards the needle so the ink is deposited during the session. Once the session is over, the tube is removed and a new one is used.

Part 3 of 6

Machine body

Home to the rechargeable battery, the TFT display that shows all the machine’s information, and much more.

Part 4 of 6

Motor

High quality, for the most demanding sessions. Fixed stroke, customised to the artist’s preference: from 2.5 mm to 4.5 mm.

Part 5 of 6

Front body

Modular design so the machine comes apart easily for cleaning and maintenance. Also handy for changing the stroke.

Part 6 of 6

Grip

Ergonomic and designed for you to adjust it to your liking, while always keeping the disposable tube’s guide in mind.
