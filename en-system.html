Our vision

A standard is only a standard if it reaches everyone.

We want any tattoo artist, anywhere in the world, to be able to work without wasting ink and without the risk of cross-contamination. That’s why we created the VK0.

  • For artists who want to focus on their work.
  • For studios that want more hygiene and efficiency.
  • For the industry, which needs a new standard.
  • For ink brands, who will be able to sell their inks pre-filled in cartridges: ink regulated from the source.
